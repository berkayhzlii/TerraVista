mAbout Mapsly

Mapsly is an independent geospatial exploration demo. It combines open map data with optional Mapillary street-level imagery search.

Mapsly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Mapillary, Meta, or OpenStreetMap. Map data and imagery remain subject to their respective providers' terms and attribution requirements.
